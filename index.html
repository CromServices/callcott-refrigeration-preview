What we do

Air conditioning, refrigeration and cool rooms

Ducted systems

Reverse cycle heating and cooling, inverter technology, zone control, from 7.1 kW to 20 kW.

Split systems

Reverse cycle, inverter technology, a single-room solution from 2 kW to 9.5 kW.

Evaporative cooling

Seasonal maintenance, plus filter pad, motor, pump and float valve replacement. Covers in grey, beige or terracotta red.

Cool rooms and freezer rooms

Custom design, construction and install — a single-use room with a swing or sliding door, or a shop-front fitout with glass door inserts and shelving. Freezer rooms, keg rooms and mobile rooms.

Cabinets and plant upgrades

Upright glass-door cabinets, stainless chiller-freezer combos and medical fridges. We also upgrade existing plant, including high-ambient condensing units, VSD compressor systems and the latest digital controls.

Maintenance and monitoring

A scheduled maintenance plan for home or business. Remote temperature monitoring of cool and freezer rooms, and compatible cabinets, using Wi-Fi, cellular and IoT platforms. HACCP room monitoring and beer reticulation systems.

About us

A small family business in Tamworth

We are a small, locally owned and operated family business. We service most of the North West and New England region, as well as many of the little places in between.

Cory is a fourth-generation Tamworthian and has been working on air-conditioning and refrigeration systems since starting his apprenticeship locally in 2001. We have a wealth of experience on domestic and commercial systems across the region.
